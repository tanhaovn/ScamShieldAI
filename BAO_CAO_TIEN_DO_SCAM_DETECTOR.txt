BAO CAO TIEN DO DU AN SCAM DETECTOR
====================================
Ngay cap nhat: 13/09/2026

1. MUC TIEU VA PHAM VI

Xay dung he thong ho tro nguoi dung nhan biet anh co dau hieu lua dao, dac
biet la screenshot tin nhan, hoa don, thong bao gia mao va duong dan thanh
toan. He thong chi dua ra canh bao va giai thich; khong thay the viec xac
minh voi ngan hang, doanh nghiep hoac co quan chuc nang.

2. THAM KHAO CAC SAN PHAM TUONG TU

Da tham khao tai lieu mo ta va pham vi chuc nang cua cac giai phap sau:

- Google Lens: nhan dien/chuyen doi van ban trong anh va tim kiem noi dung.
  Tham khao: https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/1325808
- Google Safe Browsing: kiem tra URL va canh bao trang web nguy hiem.
  Tham khao: https://safebrowsing.google.com/
- VirusTotal URL scanner: tong hop ket qua nhieu cong cu bao mat cho URL.
  Tham khao: https://docs.virustotal.com/docs/analysing
- ScamAdviser: danh gia website theo diem tin cay va cac dau hieu cua domain.
  Tham khao: https://www.scamadviser.com/check-website
- Microsoft Lens: chup tai lieu, OCR va chuyen tai lieu sang dang co the tim
  kiem/chinh sua. Tham khao: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/
  what-is-microsoft-lens-9e0fc5c4-4c98-4e3f-9c7e-4d0a5e6b6b1f

Tu cac tham khao tren, phien ban cua nhom tap trung vao bai toan cu the hon:
upload anh -> OCR tieng Viet -> tach URL -> phan tich noi dung, URL va dau
hieu chinh sua anh -> tong hop diem -> giai thich va goi y hanh dong an toan.

3. CAC TINH NANG SE XAY DUNG

DA CO TRONG DEMO:
- Dang ky, dang nhap JWT, tai khoan Google tuy chon va phan quyen user/admin.
- Upload anh, xem preview, chon danh muc scam va nhan ket qua.
- OCR tieng Viet; phan tich tu khoa, URL/domain va dau hieu so tai khoan.
- Phan tich EXIF, ELA va deepfake heuristic; co the thay bang model deepfake.
- Diem 0-100 va ba muc an_toan, nghi_ngo, nguy_hiem.
- Hien thi OCR, cum tu bi danh dau, diem thanh phan, giai thich va goi y.
- Luu lich su quet vao MySQL; xem danh muc scam.
- Trang admin cho dataset, AI models, model evaluations va system logs.
- API /health va Swagger tai /docs.

DU KIEN HOAN THIEN:
- Quan ly tu khoa scam tren giao dien admin thay vi hardcode.
- Loc, tim kiem, xuat lich su va bieu do thong ke rui ro.
- Gioi han file, kiem tra file doc hai, xu ly anh nhieu kich thuoc va nhieu
  dinh dang.
- Thong bao loading/empty/error ro rang, thay alert bang thong bao trong UI.
- Mo rong input text va URL truc tiep; hien tai luong chinh la input anh.

4. THIET KE TINH NANG OCR

INPUT:
- File anh JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP hoac BMP; co the la screenshot tin nhan,
  hoa don, ma QR kem chu, thong bao ngan hang.

QUY TRINH:
1. Backend nhan file va luu file tam trong thu muc uploads.
2. Pillow mo anh; pytesseract goi Tesseract voi lang vie+eng.
3. Tesseract dung tessdata/vie.traineddata neu co, de doc tieng Viet co dau,
   chu Latin, so tai khoan va URL.
4. Van ban duoc trim va dua vao Risk Engine. URL/domain duoc tach rieng cho
   PhishVN. Ham extract_text_with_boxes co the tra bounding box de highlight.

OUTPUT:
- extracted_text: van ban OCR.
- highlighted_terms: cum tu, URL hoac mau so dang ngo.
- Cac diem text_risk_score, visual_risk_score va risk_score tong hop.

GIOI HAN:
- Anh mo, chu nho, bi lo, nghieng, font la hoac nen phuc tap co the lam OCR
  sai. Ket qua OCR khong duoc xem la bang chung tuyet doi.
- Can bo sung bo test OCR co ground truth tieng Viet va do CER/WER truoc khi
  ket luan OCR da dat chat luong production.

5. INPUT, OUTPUT VA LOGIC GIAI THICH/GI GOI Y

INPUT CUA LOP PHAN TICH:
- Van ban OCR va cac tu/cum tu trung tu khoa.
- URL/domain tach duoc tu OCR va diem PhishVN.
- Ket qua EXIF, ELA, deepfake heuristic/model.
- Ket qua ResNet18 neu checkpoint model anh ton tai.

LOGIC:
- Risk Engine bo cong trong so cho tu khoa tieng Viet, URL dang ngo va mau
  so tai khoan; chuan hoa bo dau truoc khi so khop.
- PhishVN dung 21 dac trung lexical cua URL de phan loai phishing/legitimate.
- Visual forensics cong diem tu EXIF, ELA va deepfake; heuristic deepfake bi
  giam trong so vi do tin cay thap.
- Lop explanation_builder tao breakdown theo tung thanh phan, chon tin hieu
  co diem cao nhat lam ly do chinh va xep muc theo nguong 25/60.

OUTPUT CHO NGUOI DUNG:
- Ket luan va diem 0-100.
- Muc do: An toan tuong doi, Can kiem tra them, Nguy hiem.
- Cac thanh phan: Noi dung OCR, URL PhishVN, Dau hieu hinh anh va Model anh.
- Cum tu/URL dang ngo, ly do, cac han che cua phuong phap.
- Goi y: anh an toan thi van can than; anh nghi ngo thi xac minh kenh chinh
  thuc; anh nguy hiem thi khong nhap link, khong chuyen khoan, khong cung cap
  OTP/thong tin ca nhan va can nhac bao cao.

6. BANG CHUNG VE GIAI PHAP AI VA DANH GIA

6.1. Phuong phap da cai dat

- PhishVN v3.1.0: model HistGradientBoosting voi 21 dac trung lexical cua URL.
  Model bundled nam trong PhishVN A Time-Stamped Vietnamese URL Phishing Dat,
  co the tai lai theo temporal split. DOI: 10.17632/b97hxbxtpd.4.
- Anh phishing/legitimate: pipeline fine-tune ResNet18 pretrained tren
  ImageNet, chi mo lop phan loai cuoi, WeightedRandomSampler va chia du lieu
  70% train, 15% validation, 15% test. File code: backend/app/ai/
  train_image_model.py.
- Baseline giai thich: rule-based Risk Engine. Cac luat nay de kiem tra,
  truy vet va lam duong co so khi so sanh voi model NLP sau nay.

6.2. Cach danh gia

- URL: bao cao precision, recall, F1 va confusion matrix tren gold/silver
  temporal test; uu tien recall nhung phai theo doi false positive. Khong
  danh dong model URL la model hieu toan bo tin nhan.
- Anh: bao cao accuracy, precision, recall, F1 macro, confusion matrix tren
  test set khong dung khi train. Neu mat bang lop khac nhau, dung macro F1 va
  balanced accuracy thay vi chi dung accuracy.
- OCR: chon mot tap anh co transcription chuan, tinh CER va WER theo tung
  nhom anh: screenshot, hoa don, font nho, anh mo.
- Giai thich: danh gia voi nguoi dung bang ti le ly do duoc chap nhan, muc do
  huu ich 1-5 va ti le goi y an toan phu hop; co the luu vao
  model_evaluations.explanation_usefulness.
- Can ghi seed, phien ban dataset, cach chia tap, nguong va thoi gian chay de
  ket qua tai lap duoc. Khong dung anh trung lap giua train va test.

6.3. Nhan dinh ve tinh hop ly

Giai phap da tang la hop ly cho prototype vi ket hop OCR, URL model, rule
engine va visual forensics, dong thoi co fallback khi model khong san sang.
Tuy nhien diem tong hop hien la diem canh bao, khong phai xac suat da hieu
chinh. Truoc khi cong bo do chinh xac can chay benchmark co nhan, calibration
nguong va kiem tra false positive tren anh hop le trong thuc te.

7. CAC BUOC TAO DU LIEU TRAIN VA TEST

1. Thu thap anh tu dataset cong khai, anh tu nguyen da an danh va mau tong hop
   co kiem soat; khong thu thap thong tin ca nhan that neu khong co phep.
2. Gan nhan legitimate/phishing va nhan phu: sms_scam, fake_invoice,
   impersonation, payment_redirect; luu nguon, ngay thu thap va nguoi duyet.
3. An danh thong tin: ten, so dien thoai, email, tai khoan, dia chi va QR.
4. Kiem tra nhan boi hai nguoi; loai anh trung lap, anh loi va anh khong ro.
5. Tach theo nguon/thoi gian/chu de: train 70%, validation 15%, test 15%.
   Anh cua cung mot mau screenshot phai o cung mot split de tranh leakage.
6. Augmentation chi ap dung cho train: crop nhe, xoay nho, thay doi sang/toi
   va blur vua phai; khong lam bien doi nhan phishing.
7. Luu manifest CSV/JSON gom image_id, label, category, source, split,
   anonymized, verified va hash file.
8. Chay baseline rule-based, sau do train ResNet18/PhishVN va so sanh bang
   cac metric o muc 6.2. Luu checkpoint va report metric cung version dataset.

8. TRANG THAI DA KIEM CHUNG

Da co test tu dong trong thu muc tests:
- Dang ky, dang nhap, JWT va /me.
- /check-image tra risk_score va risk_level ngay ca khi khong co MySQL/model
  day du trong test local.
- Seed tai khoan admin va cac endpoint dataset/evaluation/log.
- Tach URL, tao dac trung PhishVN va nhan dien URL test phishing.
- Giai thich co cau truc, chi ra thanh phan co tin hieu manh nhat.
- Backend compile va frontend build da duoc xac nhan trong bao cao truoc.

Can bo sung bang chung dinh luong:
- Log ket qua chay full pytest va npm run build trong lan demo gan nhat.
- Bao cao OCR CER/WER.
- Bao cao ResNet18 tren test set va xac nhan checkpoint models/phishing_resnet18.pt.
- Bao cao PhishVN tren temporal test theo dung giao thuc cua tai lieu tham khao.

11. KE HOACH TIEP THEO

UU TIEN 1 - HOAN THIEN BANG CHUNG:
- Chay va luu report pytest/npm build.
- Tao tap OCR co ground truth; chay CER/WER.
- Chay train ResNet18 va luu accuracy, precision, recall, macro F1.
- Tao benchmark PhishVN temporal va luu confusion matrix.

UU TIEN 2 - CHAT LUONG VA UX:
- Them test upload nhieu dinh dang/kich thuoc va test phan quyen admin.
- Them loading, empty state, error state va thong bao trong giao dien.
- Hien thi bounding box OCR va bieu do diem thanh phan.
- Chuyen tu khoa sang bang scam_keywords de admin cap nhat.

UU TIEN 3 - SAN SANG TRIEN KHAI:
- Bat buoc JWT_SECRET moi, bo admin mac dinh va dung secret manager.
- Gioi han dung luong/loai file, kiem tra virus va khong phuc vu file upload
  tuy y.
- HTTPS, rate limit, logging/monitoring, backup DB va Docker/deployment.
- Calibration diem, kiem thu false positive va review an toan truoc khi goi
  la san pham production.

12. KET LUAN

Du an da co demo end-to-end co the chay: dang nhap, upload anh, OCR, phan tich
nhieu lop, luu ket qua va xem lich su. Diem manh hien tai la da co code tham
chieu AI va test cho cac luong quan trong. Phan chua du de ket luan la chat
luong production gom benchmark co nhan, metric tai lap duoc, tap OCR danh gia,
bao mat upload va checkpoint model anh da xac nhan. Buoi toi can tap trung vao
bo sung bang chung dinh luong va chot phan cong/thoi han cho cac muc tren.
